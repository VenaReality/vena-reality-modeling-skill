# Meta Case: Customer Service Efficiency Problem

## Scenario

A company observes:

"Customer service efficiency is low."

The initial assumption:

The customer service team needs optimization.

---

# Reality Examination

## Problem Definition

Is customer service efficiency the real problem?

The visible symptom:

- Slow response
- High workload
- Long resolution time

But the deeper question:

Why does customer service workload exist?

---

# Reality Capture

## Facts

Observed:

- Customer inquiries increased.
- After-sales cases increased.
- Customer service response time became longer.

---

## Constraints

Possible constraints:

- Limited customer service resources.
- Existing processes require manual handling.
- System capabilities are insufficient.
- Business rules are unclear.

---

## Variables

Potential driving factors:

- Product quality
- Merchant behavior
- Fulfillment quality
- User expectation
- After-sales rules
- Platform governance

---

## Observer Perspective

Different observers see different problems.

Customer service team:

"The workload is too high."

Product team:

"The system capability is insufficient."

Business team:

"The customer experience is declining."

Management:

"The operating efficiency is decreasing."

The problem definition changes depending on the observer.

---

## Business Essence

Customer service is not only a support function.

The deeper business mechanism includes:

- Product quality
- Merchant responsibility
- Fulfillment experience
- User trust
- Platform governance

Reducing customer service workload is not always achieved by optimizing customer service itself.

---

# Reality Model


Customer Service Pressure

=

After-sales Volume

Product / Service Quality

Fulfillment Experience

User Expectation Gap

Governance Mechanism


---

# Decision Understanding

A surface solution:

"Improve customer service efficiency."

A reality-based solution:

"Understand and reduce the factors that create unnecessary customer service demand."

The key question changes from:

"How do we make customer service faster?"

to:

"What reality creates excessive customer service needs?"
