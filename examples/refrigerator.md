# Meta Case: Refrigerator and Human Need Reality

## Scenario

A technology change appears:

A new technology can replace the traditional function of refrigeration.

The initial assumption:

"If the function is replaced, the demand disappears."

---

# Reality Examination

## Problem Definition

Does the disappearance of a product function mean the disappearance of human demand?

---

# Reality Capture

## Facts

Observed:

- Technology changes product capabilities.
- Some traditional functions may become less important.

---

## Hidden Variables

The deeper variables are not only product functions.

They include:

- Human convenience needs
- Food preservation needs
- Lifestyle patterns
- Safety expectations
- Behavioral habits

---

## Human Factor

People do not buy products only because of functions.

They buy solutions to underlying needs.

A product function is only one expression of a deeper reality.

---

## Business Essence

The essence of a product is not:

"The function it provides."

The essence is:

"The human problem it solves."

---

# Reality Model


Product Function

≠

Human Need

Function Change

≠

Demand Disappearance


---

# Decision Understanding

A common reasoning error:

"Technology replaces a function, therefore the market disappears."

Reality-based reasoning:

"Understand whether the underlying human need still exists and how it may transform."

The question changes from:

"Does this function still exist?"

to:

"What human reality creates this demand?"
